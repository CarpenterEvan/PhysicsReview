Event space: abstract space that contains the set of all ``events''. Events just objects with well defined variables like momenta, \(\eta\), invariant mass. These variables gives the space structure, since you can sort the space and calculate distances between the points. 
\\
Process: something that generates events. Typically split into background and signal processes, which depend on the analysis at hand. 
\\ 
Samples: Each sample is a ({\tt.root}) file containing MC generated (or measured data) for specific detector or simulation conditions. Detector conditions might be time of collection, and simulation conditions might be for a specific process. Sometimes collections of samples, sorted by their representing process, are still called samples. 
\\
Region: subsection of event space that is restricted by cuts on chosen variables.
