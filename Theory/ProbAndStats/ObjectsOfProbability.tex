\subsection{Objects of Probability}
Everything happens in the sample space, a space of all outcomes for an experiment. 
A random variable \(X\) is a quantity that can be measured, all possible values the variable can take are defined in a sample space, and the outcome of the measurement will result in \(X\) taking on one of the predefined values. The most common example is a coin flip, the sample space is \(\{\text{heads},\text{tails}\}\)
